Quick start

  1. Press stop on the module, then switch off your case. The module writes to the card when you stop the sequencer, so stop first.
  2. Take the SD card out and put it in your computer. It shows up as a drive (on a Mac often called NO NAME).
  3. Copy all files on the card to a folder on your computer. That is your backup.
  4. Click Open, or drag files from Finder or Explorer onto this page. You can open several files at once; each file goes to its own tab.
  5. Make your changes and click Save (Ctrl/Cmd+S). In Chrome and Edge this writes straight back to the file on the card.
  6. Eject the card properly, put it back in the module and switch on. The module reads the card when it starts.

All files are plain text. The module writes them with Windows line endings (CRLF). This editor keeps the line endings of the file you open, and uses CRLF for a new bank.

The module reads the card when it starts. It writes patterns, settings and MIDI maps whenever you stop the sequencer. So only swap the card with the module switched off, and stop the sequencer before you switch off.

Factory reset. Delete all files from the card, or use an empty FAT32 card. The module writes factory files at the next start.

Patterns

A bank has 24 patterns (PTN0–PTN23) for each track. Kick and Snare patterns are 4 bars (64 steps). Hihat 1, Hihat 2, Perc 1 and Perc 2 are 1 bar (16 steps) that loops. One step is a 16th note in 4/4, before shuffle.

  • Pick a pattern with the numbered strip of a track. The strip at the top picks the same pattern for all tracks. A dot means the pattern has steps.
  • Click a step to turn it on or off. Drag across steps to set or clear several at once. With the keyboard: Tab to a step and press Enter.
  • Copy and Paste work across tracks. Pasting 4 bars into a 1-bar track takes the first bar. Pasting 1 bar into a 4-bar track repeats it.
  • Bar 1 → 2–4 copies the first bar of Kick or Snare to the other three bars.
  • M mutes and S solos a track in the preview. Click a track name to hear its sound.
  • Bank on module sets the file name: bank 5 is PatternBank004.txt. New starts an empty bank with that name.
  • A bank must be active to be reachable with the ◄ Bank ► buttons. Turn it on in the Config tab.

Preview

The preview plays the bank with simple synthesized sounds. It is an approximation of the module:

  • Shuffle: steps fall on the positions of the shuffle display in the manual, per beat of 4 steps. The manual itself calls that display a visual approximation.
  • Timing (Trip, Shake, Push, Clave) is not in the preview.
  • Feeling: Flam and Slop delay kick and snare a little, Slop also the hihats. Rolls replaces Hihat 2 with a burst.
  • Linear: snare beats kick, and both beat the hihats. Since firmware 157 the module also applies this to Perc 1 and 2, and lets Hihat 2 beat Hihat 1 and Perc 2 beat Perc 1 (Snare > Kick > Hihat 2 > Hihat 1, and Snare > Kick > Perc 2 > Perc 1). The preview does not do that yet.
  • Tempo: 15–250 bpm, like the module.

Config

Settings for the whole machine. The module reads them when it starts. Switches are 1 (on) or 0 (off) in the file.

The module ships with banks 1–4 active. Firmware 157 and 160 added factory banks 5 and 6; you may have to activate those yourself. The last line of the file shows the firmware build.

Envelopes

The two Env outputs give a tempo-synced 10 V CV. On the module, press and release the Subdiv encoder for 2 seconds to enter the ENV options. The upper encoder row (starting at Kick) sets ENV 1, the lower row (starting at Perc 1) sets ENV 2: waveform, clock divider, lower and upper boundary.

Waveforms

Clock dividers

An envelope either follows the bar or a beat, or restarts with a trigger of one of the tracks. It follows the shuffle, timing and feeling you select.

Range

  • x4 to P2 share one range. The upper boundary is the maximum and the lower boundary the offset; the envelope moves between the two. In the file: ENV1_LFO_VEL (upper) and ENV1_LFO_LOW_VEL (lower).
  • :bd to :P2 have a setting per instrument. Upper is the accented velocity and lower the non-accented velocity. The envelope moves between zero and upper on an accent, and between zero and lower otherwise. In the file: ENV1_KICK_VEL and ENV1_KICK_LOW_VEL, and so on.
  • Values run from 0 to 127. On the module one LED is 8 steps.

The plot in the Envelopes tab is a sketch of shape and range, not a measurement of the output.

Default state

The module saves its current state in MachineState.txt when you stop the sequencer, and starts with that state. Edit it to choose how the module starts.

When you play and stop on the module, it overwrites this file. WRITE_MACHINE_STATE in MachineConfig.txt probably turns that off, so the module always starts with your state. That setting is not documented; check it on your module.

Undocumented values

The manual describes the settings, but not the numbers in the files. This editor assumes that values count from 0, in the order of the manual and the panel. Pattern numbers (0 is PTN0) and MIDI channels in the MIDI maps (0 is channel 1) work that way too. Fields that rely on this assumption are marked inferred.

To check a value: set it here, save, start the module and compare with the display. For example, set ENV 1 to Ramp down and check that the first waveform is lit in the ENV options.

Shortcuts

  • Space start/stop the preview (Patterns tab)
  • Ctrl/Cmd+S save the file of the current tab
  • Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo (Patterns tab)
  • ← → switch tabs when a tab has focus

Troubleshooting

  • A bank does not show up on the module. Activate it in the Config tab. Check the file name: PatternBank plus three digits, .txt, in the root of the card.
  • My changes are gone. The module rewrites the card when you stop the sequencer. Edit the card with the module switched off.
  • Hidden files on the card. macOS writes files like ._PatternBank004.txt, .Spotlight-V100 and .fseventsd to the card. The module probably ignores them. To remove the ._ files, run dot_clean -m "/Volumes/NO NAME" in Terminal (use the name of your card).
  • Saving fails. The card may be locked (switch on the side of the card), or the browser lost permission. The editor then downloads the file instead.
